## Risks / concerns

- Tata Steel delivered an improved performance in a seasonally softer quarter, despite significant cost inflation across the
value chain due to the ongoing conflict in West Asia.
- The conflict in West Asia continued to
disrupt global trade flows, with vessels rerouted around the Cape of Good Hope, extending transit times by 10 – 14 days,
increasing fuel consumption by almost 40%, and keeping war-risk insurance costs materially above pre-conflict levels.
- However, the timing and outcome of the
process remain uncertain.
- Domestic demand remains weak, with automotive and construction activity
expected to decline by 2 – 4% in the current calendar year, while import pressure remains elevated.
- Our interest cover is more around 5x plus, so I don't think there is any concern
around that, because we will be spending more capex.
